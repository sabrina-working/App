Add App to Phone Home Screen

On iPhone / iPad (Safari):

1. Look for the Share icon — it can appear in different spots depending on your iPhone/iOS version, so check for any of these:

• A square with an arrow pointing up (usually at the bottom of the screen)

• Three dots (•••) at the bottom or top of the screen

• Three lines (☰) menu, usually top-right

Tap whichever one you see — they all open the same Share menu.

2. In the Share menu, scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".

3. Tap Add in the top-right corner. It will now launch like a native full-screen app!

On Android (Chrome / Edge):

1. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top-right.

2. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

3. Confirm to install. The app icon will appear on your phone desktop!
